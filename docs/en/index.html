THE FASTEST WAY

Right in Finder.

You don't even need to open Treco. Hold ⇧ Shift while dragging a file and the actions pop up around your cursor.

  1. Select one or more files in Finder.
  2. Start dragging and hold ⇧ Shift.
  3. Drop on what you want: convert, compress, edit, caption or rename.

The result shows up next to the original, which is never moved or deleted.

What it does

Video & audio

  • Convert (MP4, MOV, MP3, WAV…)
  • Compress to the size you want
  • Trim, crop, rotate, mirror
  • Faster or slower without deepening the voice
  • Mute, adjust volume
  • Caption videos and transcribe speech
  • Cut silences from speech, with the breathing room you choose
  • Clean up voice: hiss, noise and echo
  • BPM and key of a song
  • Record voice, screen, or both

Images

  • Convert (JPG, PNG, HEIC, WEBP…)
  • Compress
  • Scan document: a photo becomes a straight, clean PDF
  • Photos to PDF
  • Slides to video
  • Remove background, read text, blur faces
  • Watermark

Documents

  • Merge and split PDFs
  • Add or remove a PDF password

  • Summarize a page with the Mac's own AI

Utilities

How to install

  1. Download Treco (.dmg file).
  2. Open it and drag Treco into Applications.
  3. Open Treco from Applications.
  4. The first time, macOS says it can't verify the app, because it's free and not from the App Store. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. You only need to do this once.

Requires

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 13 or newer.
  • Translate and Summarize page need macOS 26 (Summarize also needs Apple Intelligence turned on).

Permissions macOS may ask for

  • Screen Recording: to record the screen and for scrolling screenshots.
  • Microphone: to record your voice.
  • Downloads, Desktop and Documents folders: for Clean Downloads, Space lens and duplicate files.
  • Control Finder: only when uninstalling App Store apps.
  • Full Disk Access (optional): lets Uninstall remove the data folders macOS protects.

Privacy

Treco has no account, collects no data and never sends your files anywhere. It only goes online when you ask (downloading a video, reading a page to summarize, testing your internet, downloading the captions model) and, once a day, to check for a new version.
